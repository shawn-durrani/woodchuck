- A dado, groove or rabbet, or the dado of a dado and rabbet, can stop
  short of one or both edges of the part it's cut into, so its end doesn't
  show on a visible edge, such as a dado stopped 10 mm from a side's
  front. The part that sits in it keeps its place and size, and its corner
  is notched to match. The cut list says "stopped 10 mm from the front"
  and lists each notch, the workshop drawings size the stop and the notch,
  and the see-through view shows the housing ending short of the edge. The
  checks refuse a stop that leaves no housing and warn when one leaves less
  than half of it or under 6 mm of wood before the edge. Claude adds a stop
  with `add_joint`, and the worked example of each joint that can stop has
  a Stopped switch.
