- A Domino joint cuts every mortise in one piece on the joiner's wider
  setting, the one nearest the front too, so you can line the parts up at
  glue-up. Every mortise in the other piece is as wide as the tenon, which
  locates the joint. The loose piece is the host, whose face or edge takes
  the joint, unless the joint says the guest with `loose`. The play is
  6 mm, the DF 500's middle setting, for any number of Dominos, so a joint
  of one or two gets it too. A fit of 10 takes the widest setting, and 0
  cuts both pieces tight. The cut list and the workshop drawings say which
  mortises have play, and the checks measure each at the length it's cut.
