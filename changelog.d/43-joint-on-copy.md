- Claude can join one shelf of an evenly spaced row on its own, such as a
  drawer divider housed into the underside of the second shelf. The joint
  names that copy, such as `shelf#2`, and the shelves' own joints still
  repeat on every one. The cut list gives that shelf a row of its own,
  named for it, and the workshop drawings give it a sheet. A count that
  drops below the copy's number shows the joint as a problem to fix, and a
  count that rises keeps it where it was.
