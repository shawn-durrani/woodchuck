- The workshop drawings place each Domino mortise by its centre, the way
  the joiner lines up on a pencil mark, on the chains and in the notes. A
  note gives which way the mortise runs, and for a mortise in an end or an
  edge, how far in its centre sits, which is the fence height. A part with
  work spread evenly along it, such as a rail with braces, is set out from
  its middle, with the distance from the end to the middle and the chain
  running through it.
