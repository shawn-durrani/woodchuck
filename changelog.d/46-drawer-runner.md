- Drawers without slides run on wooden runners. When slides don't fit or
  aren't wanted, Claude makes each runner a timber strip tagged runner, so
  it's on the cut list with its size, and the cut list says to wax it. The
  checks make sure a drawer on runners can slide, with at least 0.5 mm of
  clear space beside it and over it, and a groove a little wider than its
  runner. The hardware list holds only what you buy, so a placeholder such
  as wax glides is refused, and one already in a design gets a warning.
