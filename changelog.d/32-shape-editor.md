- You can shape a part by hand. A panel's Edit tab has a Shape section
  that lists its cuts in workshop words, with any problems under each, and
  buttons to slope an edge, cut a round, rectangular or slot hole, or take
  a notch out of a corner for a toe kick. Each starts somewhere you can see
  at once, such as a slope down to two thirds of the part's height, and
  its fields take numbers, sums and faces such as `@drawer_box_back.top`.
  While you type, the model shows the change as a ghost, with the new angle
  and end heights or the reason the design won't take it. Leaving the field
  makes it as one undo step, and Esc puts it back. The cut list shows each
  shaped part's notes under its name.
