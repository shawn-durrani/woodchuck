- Claude's replies draw a Markdown table, such as a cut list, as a real
  table. A wide one scrolls sideways inside its message, so a phone shows
  it too. Claude knows the side panel's five tabs and what each holds, so
  it can tell you the cut list is on Make and the problems are on Check.
  Another app, such as Crossband, can open a tab with `woodchuck_view`, so
  "show me the cut list" opens Make without asking Claude.
