- Another chat app, such as Crossband, can change a size the design
  already has straight away, without waiting for Claude.
  `woodchuck_set_param` sets parameters such as the top's length as one
  undo step, mid-build too. Its reply gives each old and new value, the
  sizes that moved with it and every new problem. A change of more than
  20%, or to zero or less, waits for the woodworker's yes, in case a number
  was misheard. `woodchuck_design` reads the design's parameters, parts and
  sizes in a few short lines.
