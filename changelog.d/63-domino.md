- Parts can be joined with Festool Dominos. The new domino joint cuts a
  matching mortise in each part for every tenon, spread along the joint the
  way dowels are, and changes no part's size. It picks a tenon from the DF
  500's range to suit the stock, with depths the machine can cut and play
  in the host's mortises for lining up a long joint. The cut list and the
  workshop drawings place every mortise, and the hardware list counts the
  beech tenons to buy, each a library part with Festool's figures. The
  checks catch a size Festool doesn't make, a mortise with under 5 mm of
  wood round it, and mortises that run into each other, a cut or another
  joint. Claude uses Dominos when your workshop lists a Domino joiner, and
  dowels when it doesn't.
