- Tell Woodchuck the boards and sheets you already have, and the cut
  layout cuts from those first and lists only what's left to buy. A part
  narrower than one of your boards is ripped from it, two side by side
  when they fit, and you can say which widths your yard sells so bought
  boards are ripped too. The new cutting plan prints every board on one
  page to take to the saw: each with a letter, its parts in cutting
  order, its rips and offcuts, and the cut list names the board each part
  comes from. Claude asks what wood you have when you're ready to cut,
  and another chat can read the plan and set your stock over MCP.
